# How AI Coding Tools Are Changing the Future of Software Companies

## Project Overview

This project explores how AI-assisted software development is changing the software industry by making complex applications more accessible to independent developers and smaller teams. As major software companies increasingly integrate AI into their products, these same technologies may enable new competitors to challenge established software and subscription-based business models.

Using **Adobe vs. ArtCraft** as a case study, we examine how AI-assisted coding tools can help developers create free, open-source alternatives to proprietary software, while considering the limitations of AI-generated code and the continued importance of human oversight.

## Research Materials

Our research is divided into three main areas:

### 1. AI-Assisted Software Development
Research into tools such as GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, and Cursor, focusing on how AI assists with writing, debugging, and testing software.

[View AI-Assisted Development Research](research/ai-development.md)

### 2. AI's Impact on Software Companies
An examination of how AI could lower barriers to software development, increase competition, and affect proprietary software and subscription-based business models.

This research incorporates findings from Google's 2025 DORA report and Deloitte's 2026 Digital Media Trends research.

[View Software Industry Impact Research](research/software-industry-impact.md)

### 3. Adobe vs. ArtCraft Case Study
A comparison of Adobe's integration of generative AI with ArtCraft, a project using AI-assisted development to create free, open-source alternatives to popular Adobe applications.

The research examines ArtCraft's capabilities, limitations, and potential implications for established software companies.

[View Adobe vs. ArtCraft Research](research/adobe-artcraft.md)

## Conclusion

Our presentation examines the potential impact of AI-assisted development on established software companies. While AI tools may make developing complex applications more accessible, the Adobe vs. ArtCraft case study highlights the ongoing importance of software reliability, testing, maintenance, and human expertise.

Ultimately, the project raises an important question: **If comparable software becomes available for free, how will established companies continue to justify the cost of their products?**
