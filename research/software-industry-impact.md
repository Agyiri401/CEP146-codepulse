## 1. Lowering Barriers to Software Development

According to Google's 2025 DORA report, 90% of surveyed technology professionals reported using AI at work, while over 80% believed it increased their productivity (pp. 3-4).

These findings suggest that AI-assisted tools are becoming increasingly integrated into software development.

**Potential implications:**
- AI may reduce the time needed for certain development tasks.
- Independent developers and smaller teams may be able to accomplish more with fewer resources.
- Lower development costs could make it easier for new competitors to enter the software industry.

**Analysis:** Although the report provides evidence of increased perceived productivity, it does not directly establish that AI has reduced the cost of developing entire software products or eliminated barriers to market entry.

## 2. Increased Competition for Established Companies

As AI tools become more accessible, smaller developers may find it easier to prototype and develop software that competes with products offered by larger companies.

Established software companies have traditionally benefited from significant financial resources, experienced development teams, and established technical infrastructure.

AI could reduce some of these advantages by making certain development activities more efficient.

**Potential implications:**
- Increased competition from smaller companies and independent developers.
- Faster development of competing products.
- Greater availability of alternative software.
- Increased pressure on established companies to innovate.

**Analysis:** These are possible industry consequences, not outcomes directly demonstrated by the DORA report.

## 3. Implications for Proprietary Software and Subscription Models

If AI reduces the resources needed to develop competing applications, proprietary software companies could face greater competition from lower-cost or open-source alternatives.

This could encourage companies to reconsider how they price their products and demonstrate their value to customers.

**Potential implications:**
- Increased competition over software pricing.
- More accessible open-source alternatives.
- Greater emphasis on reliability, compatibility, customer support, and specialized features.

**Analysis:** While the DORA report does not directly measure subscription revenue, software pricing, or customer switching behavior, Deloitte's Fall 2026 Digital Media Trends research found that nearly two-thirds of consumers expressed frustration with rising entertainment subscription prices, while more than one-third reported recently reducing their subscriptions due to financial concerns.

## 4. Benefits for Established Software Companies

AI-assisted development could also benefit large software companies rather than simply threatening them.

Google's DORA research identifies AI as an amplifier of existing organizational capabilities. Companies with effective development practices and infrastructure may be better positioned to benefit from AI adoption.

**Potential benefits:**
- Increased developer productivity.
- Faster software delivery.
- More efficient development workflows.
- Greater ability to integrate AI into existing products.

The report also associates AI adoption with greater software delivery **instability**, suggesting companies must balance speed with quality.

## 5. Limitations and Challenges

The report found that approximately 30% of respondents had little or no trust in AI-generated code.

This suggests that code verification, testing, and human oversight remain important even as AI tools become more capable.

**Implications:**
- AI-generated software still requires testing and maintenance.
- Rapid code generation can introduce additional quality-control challenges.
- Established companies may retain advantages through security, reliability, infrastructure, and long-term support.

## 6. Key Research Conclusion

AI-assisted development could change competition in the software industry by reducing some of the resources and effort needed to create software.

However, the 2025 DORA report suggests that AI alone does not guarantee successful software development. Organizational processes, technical infrastructure, and quality-control practices remain essential.

**Overall, AI may reduce some barriers to creating software, but developing and maintaining reliable products could remain an important competitive advantage for established companies.**
